AILA

Before you begin

AILA is an AI-powered communication practice tool. Please review these terms before continuing.

Purpose

AILA helps you practice professional conversations in a safe environment. It is not a substitute for mental health support, legal advice, or HR guidance.

Privacy

Your conversations are processed by Groq AI. Do not share personal identifying information, confidential company data, or sensitive personal details.

Data retention

Conversations are not saved between sessions. Audio is transcribed server-side and immediately discarded. Imported scenarios are stored locally in your browser.

Hands-free microphone

After you enter the studio, AILA requests microphone access and listens locally for speech so turns can start and stop automatically. Only detected speech clips are sent for transcription, and you can turn hands-free mode off at any time.

AI processing

Responses are AI-generated and may be imperfect. Use them as practice prompts, not authoritative guidance.

Appropriate use

Do not submit sensitive personal data, regulated health information, or confidential information. You are responsible for how you use the app and for reviewing AI-generated content critically.
